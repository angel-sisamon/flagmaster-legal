Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play every classic mode without signing in. Rankings, friends, duels, tournaments and stars use your Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone) identity, which creates your player profile automatically.

My name doesn't appear in the rankings

Make sure you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone: Settings → Game Center) and that you have an internet connection. Your score is uploaded when the game ends.

Can I play with friends on another platform?

Yes. Rankings, duels and tournaments are shared between Android and iPhone. Add your friends with their friend code (Profile → ➕).

I play on two devices and see two profiles

Each Google Play Games or Game Center account has its own profile. If you play with two different accounts (for example an Android phone and an iPhone), you will have two separate profiles.

How do I change my country?

Go to Profile → ✏️ Edit profile and choose your country.

How do I delete my account?

iPhone: Settings → Account → Delete my account. Android: email us from the button above with the subject "Delete my FlagMaster data" and your player name or friend code. More details in the Privacy Policy.

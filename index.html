Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Applies to FlagMaster on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store)

1. Who we are

FlagMaster ("the App") is a free flags and geography quiz game developed by Angel Sisamon ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, write to angel.sisa91@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • We only collect what is needed to run the game: your game identity, your profile and your scores.
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data.
  • We do not use your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time (see section 8).

3. Data we collect and why

a) Game identity

When you sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) we receive your player ID, your public player name and, on Android, your avatar image URL. We use them to create your player profile and to unlock achievements. Signing in is optional: you can play without it, but rankings, friends, duels, tournaments and stars require it.

b) Player profile and game data

Stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): player name, avatar, friend code, friends list, country and continent, game statistics, scores, achievements, trophies, stars balance, duel and tournament results, and the platform and version of the App.

c) Approximate country

To place you in the country rankings, the App asks the service country.is which country your IP address belongs to. We only store the resulting country code; we do not store your IP address. You can change your country in your profile.

d) Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used (screens opened, game modes played, app version, device type and approximate region) and to fix problems. This data is aggregated and is not used for advertising.

e) Data stored only on your device

Your settings, language, local progress and scheduled reminders are stored on your device. Notifications are local: we do not use a push notification server.

4. What other players can see

Your player name, avatar or initial, country flag, scores, streaks and trophies are visible to other players in rankings, duels and tournaments. Your friend code is only shown to you and to whoever you share it with. Other players never see which platform (Android or iPhone) you play on.

5. Legal basis (EEA / UK users)

We process your data to provide the game you ask for (performance of a contract) and, for usage statistics, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it.

6. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics) — servers may be located in the United States.
  • Google Play Games Services (Android).
  • Apple Game Center (iOS).
  • country.is (approximate country from IP).

We only disclose data when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

7. Retention

We keep your profile and game data while you use the App. Daily and weekly ranking entries are kept so that past rankings and trophies remain consistent, until you delete your account.

8. Deleting your account and data

  • iPhone: open the App → Settings → Account → Delete my account. Your profile, scores, stars, friends, duels and progress are deleted immediately.
  • Android (or if you cannot open the App): email angel.sisa91@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FlagMaster data" and your player name or friend code (shown in your profile). We delete all your data within 30 days and confirm it by email.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes the data stored on your device.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it at the same address.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules on our servers. Account deletion requires a signature from Game Center that proves you own the account.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the App.

12. Contact

angel.sisa91@gmail.com · Support page
